Privacy policy

OpenClaw Gmail Integration is a personal tool operated by its owner, Gustav Fredrikson, for his own Google accounts only.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent the tool reads Gmail message metadata and snippets (sender, subject, date, preview) from the owner's own mailboxes.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to produce reminders for the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Storage

OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted on a server controlled by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
