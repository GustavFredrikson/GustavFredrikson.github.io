Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

This policy covers a personal tool operated by its owner, Gustav Fredrikson, for his own Google accounts only. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent the tool reads Gmail message metadata and snippets (sender, subject, date, preview), and reads and edits events in the owner's own Google Calendar.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to produce reminders and manage the owner's own calendar. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Storage

OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted on a server controlled by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
